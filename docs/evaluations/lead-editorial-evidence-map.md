## States and evidence kinds

**Planned** names a future check; **implemented** means source exists without accepted execution. **PASS/FAIL** names an actual outcome at an exact source head. **Not run** has no result. **Effect unconfirmed** means provider/human literary judgment is absent even when contract checks pass. Closed/Done denotes delivery, not quality.

Kinds: prompt inclusion, deterministic runtime, offline-tool mechanics, static documents, actual provider output, and human judgment. A string check does not prove provider behavior; a deterministic reply does not prove creative effect. Historical executed heads, current source validity and new gaps are kept separate.
